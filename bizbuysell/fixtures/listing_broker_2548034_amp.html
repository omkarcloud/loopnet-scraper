Business Description

Retiring seller. Professional route management remains in place after

After years of building this route, the seller is retiring. That's the whole reason it's on the market.

What they built is a network of about 106 ATM locations spread across the Mid-Atlantic, producing around $282,000 a year in cash flow. The asking price is $1,300,000.

Here's where the machines are:

West Virginia. Maryland. Virginia. The Washington DC market. And a few surrounding areas.

That spread works in two ways. The locations are close enough together to service efficiently, and spread across enough markets that no single area carries the whole business.

Every location is already placed, earning, and backed by a merchant relationship with real transaction history.

The daily work is covered by a professional management company. They handle cash loading, equipment service, maintenance, and merchant relations, and that setup continues after the sale. It's built as a managed investment, so it isn't meant to be turned into a self-run route.

As the owner, you'd focus on performance. Reading reports, tracking results, and deciding where the portfolio goes next.

Where it could go next: more merchant placements inside markets it already covers, and picking up compatible routes in the region.

This fits an investor who wants established, managed income in a region with room to build on.

Detailed Information
Employees:
2 Full-time
Facilities:
The portfolio is made up of roughly 106 active ATM locations throughout the Mid-Atlantic. Included in the sale: installed equipment, merchant locations, transaction history, reporting systems, and the operating setup behind it. There's no real estate or storefront. Location-level detail and full financials are shared with qualified buyers during due diligence.
Competition:
The region has a mix of small independent operators and larger ATM providers. What sets this portfolio apart is time already invested. The locations, merchant relationships, and operating history are in place, which would take years to put together from zero.
Growth & Expansion:
Two clear paths. Add merchant locations within the current markets, where the service coverage already exists. Or acquire compatible routes nearby to widen the footprint.
Financing:

None

Support & Training:
The buyer will be trained on portfolio reporting, financial performance, and the ownership side of the business. The route remains under professional management after the sale and can't be converted to a self-managed route.
