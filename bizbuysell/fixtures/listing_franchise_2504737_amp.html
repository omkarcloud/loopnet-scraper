Business Description

Corporate, Wedding & Event Catering | Repeat Clients

Turnkey specialty catering and food business located in a desirable San Diego market with limited direct competition. The business specializes in premium charcuterie boards, boxes, grazing spreads, and sandwich boards designed for corporate events, celebrations, gifting, and everyday entertaining.

The operation includes a fully equipped commercial kitchen with industrial refrigeration, preparation equipment, and streamlined systems already in place. The business benefits from growing consumer demand for convenient, elevated catering options and a modern, visually driven product that performs well for repeat business and social media marketing.

With no other dedicated storefront charcuterie competitors in the immediate area, the business is positioned for continued growth through corporate catering, local partnerships, and expansion into nearby territories. Additional upside exists through the potential acquisition of other nearby locations for a multi-unit operator.
