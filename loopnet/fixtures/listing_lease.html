    Property Overview

    CPLA AT 11340 W OLYMPIC BLVD offers newly reimagined boutique office space in the heart of West Los Angeles, just moments from the vibrant Sawtelle dining corridor. This three-story, courtyard-style property features tranquil open-air walkways, landscaped grounds, and outdoor seating that create a rare, zen-like urban workspace. Renovated suites include efficient layouts with private offices, conference rooms, and employee kitchens, while secured covered and street-level parking add everyday convenience. Ideally positioned near the 405 and 10 freeways, Commerce Plaza provides both accessibility and a refined professional environment. Enjoy proximity to numerous amenities along the bustling Sawtelle restaurant corridor near the 405 and 10 freeways. Tenants have temperature control, and HVAC is available Monday through Friday, from 8:00 am to 6:00 pm, and Saturday between 8:00 am and 1:00 pm. Additionally, benefit from responsive on-site maintenance and property management.

    About Olympic Corridor

    The Olympic Corridor sits at the center of Los Angeles’ Westside. Adjacent to the Interstates 10 and 405, the area has some of the best transportation access in Greater Los Angeles. The opening of the Expo Line expansion from Culver City to Santa Monica in 2016 was a big win for the Olympic Corridor, making it possible for workers to travel on the line to points between Santa Monica and Downtown. Retail options are strong in the Olympic Corridor, with a plethora of dining and shopping options. The Sawtelle Corridor, also known as Little Osaka, has become one of the hotspots for restaurants on the Westside.

    While the area has some of the more expensive asking rates in Greater Los Angeles, it typically offers some discount to nearby neighborhoods like Santa Monica, Marina del Rey, Venice, and Century City. Relative value on the Westside is a selling point.

    Tenancy is diverse, with a mix of professional services firms as well as tech and media companies. Neighbors include Riot Games, Hulu, Oracle, Snapchat, Bird, Starz, Lionsgate, and Skydance Media. The area offers a variety of space options for office tenants, including high-rise office towers, mid-rise creative office product, and industrial conversions.
