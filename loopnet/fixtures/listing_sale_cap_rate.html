    Executive Summary

    9775-9777 Santa Monica Boulevard offers an opportunity to own a street retail property in the Beverly Hills Golden Triangle, one of the world's most prestigious shopping districts. The single-story, multi-tenant building sits on Santa Monica Boulevard, just east of Wilshire, where the Golden Triangle meets Century City. Its boutique scale remains highly functional for restaurant, luxury-adjacent retail, showroom, beauty, wellness, and service users seeking prominent boulevard exposure. Spanning 1,700 square feet, the building features 50 feet of frontage and a striking glass-and-concrete façade. Two storefronts provide full-height glass exposure and separate entrances. Tempura Endo, a Michelin Guide restaurant with roots in Kyoto dating to 1910, occupies one space, while a 750-square-foot suite offers availability for a boutique retailer, showroom, or owner/user presence in the district. Few retail locations offer such proximity to Beverly Hills' luxury retail and hospitality anchors. Rodeo Drive is within walking distance, while The Peninsula, Waldorf Astoria, Beverly Wilshire, Beverly Hilton, and Century City are all minutes away, with 44,000 vehicles passing the property daily. A Walk Score of 96, nearby public parking, and access to the nearby City of Beverly Hills public parking structures and monthly parking options further support convenience for customers and employees. Continued investment across the Golden Triangle reinforces the area's long-term appeal. Hermès completed a record-setting acquisition on Rodeo Drive, Rosewood Residences recently opened on Santa Monica Boulevard, and One Beverly Hills is bringing an Aman hotel and residences to the immediate area. Further enhancing connectivity, Metro's D Line Extension and the Wilshire/Rodeo station, which serves both the Golden Triangle and Century City, are expected to open in spring 2027. Note: Full financial details are available in the Offering Memorandum. Property tours are available by appointment only and should be coordinated through the listing team.
