    Executive Summary

    The Garage by Dennis Collins: private car garage condos in West Plano The Garage introduces a different kind of ownership opportunity in North Dallas: a private community created for entrepreneurs, executives, collectors and automotive enthusiasts who want more than a place to store cars. There is no shortage of luxury car condo developments these days. Most of them go big: dozens upon dozens of units, hundreds of thousands of square feet, and a drive you have to plan around. The Garage was built on a different idea: the right number of condos on the right-size campus, on an infill site close to where owners already live, work and spend their weekends. Just 35 private car garage condos in three buildings, arranged around a private, turfed motor court at Tennyson Parkway and W Spring Creek Parkway. It’s less a storage complex and more a small club, where the person parked next to you may be as interesting as the car they arrived in. Delivered Premium Ready Condos range from about 1,700 to 2,500 square feet, with comfortable capacity for 6–7 cars in most. Every condo has an upper level, offered as either a front-facing mezzanine open to the showroom or a full two-story floor plate. Most have a private balcony over the motor court. Every condo has glass on both sides: a glass garage door onto the drive and, on most condos, pocketing glass sliders onto the turfed court. Many of the expensive, time-consuming improvements are already done. Depending on the condo, this includes structural second floors, finished high-end ADA restrooms, architectural steel staircases, LVP second-floor flooring, plumbing rough-ins for a future bar or kitchenette, commercial HVAC, fiber infrastructure, EV charging conduit, upgraded lighting, fire sprinklers and insulated demising walls. The location The Garage puts owners in the heart of North Dallas, not miles away in an industrial corridor. Legacy West and the Shops at Legacy are about a mile north, and there is direct access to the Dallas North Tollway and the President George Bush Turnpike. A Porsche Experience Center has also been approved for the same intersection. The community At the gate is the very first Coffee Walk cafe, hand-designed by Dennis Collins and Landmark Hospitality Group, giving owners a unique combination of coffee, convenience and camaraderie. Above it, the member-only Owners Lounge has a chef’s kitchen, an executive conference room and a terrace. There’s also a private mail and package area, on-site detailing, vehicle prep for pick-up, and a gated campus with controlled access and security cameras. Built by a collector Dennis Collins has spent four decades around collector cars and the people who own them, first through Collins Bros Jeep, founded in 1984, and now through Coffee Walk, his weekly YouTube series with more than 650,000 subscribers. His involvement goes beyond putting a name on the development: he is bringing his experience, relationships, standards and the Coffee Walk brand into the community. That creates something another garage building can’t reproduce: access, energy and a calendar of owner gatherings, automotive events, guest collections and special guests. A growing network West Plano is the first. Murphy follows, pre-selling Q2 2027, then Southlake, pre-selling Q4 2027, with more to come. Buy in Plano and you’re getting in early on a network that grows bigger and better connected with every site that opens. For the right owner, The Garage becomes part showroom, part office, part clubhouse, part investment and part calling card. It’s not simply somewhere to store what you’ve built, but a place to enjoy it. Now pre-selling. Contact the listing team for current availability, pricing, floor plans, specifications and private tours.
