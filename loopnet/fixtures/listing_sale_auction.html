    Executive Summary

    Newmark has been exclusively retained to arrange for the sale of a package of commercial condominiums located at 11 East Broadway, New York, NY (the “Property”). The offering consists of the ground floor retail condominium and basement, along with the second and third floor office condominiums, totaling 16,831 rentable square feet. Positioned along the highly trafficked East Broadway corridor at the nexus of Chinatown and the Lower East Side, the Property benefits from exceptional visibility along one of Manhattan’s most active pedestrian retail corridors. The 9,394 RSF retail condominium occupies the ground floor and basement and offers prominent street presence along East Broadway, a major commercial thoroughfare known for its dense retail concentration, strong neighborhood foot traffic, and constant tourist activity. Above, the second and third floor office condominiums total 7,436 RSF and provide efficient, full-floor layouts well suited for a variety of commercial, medical, educational, or community facility uses. Together, the three condominium units function as a cohesive commercial package within the building while remaining legally distinct condominium interests. The Property was formerly occupied by HSBC, which they used as a single integrated unit with a banking floor on grade, safety deposit boxes on the lower level, and executive offices on the second and third floors with interconnecting stairways and an elevator. Located in the heart of Chinatown, the Property is surrounded by a vibrant mix of retail, restaurants, professional services, and cultural institutions that serve both the local community and visitors from across New York City. The area benefits from strong transit connectivity via the East Broadway F train, Brooklyn Bridge City Hall & Canal Street 6 train, as well as proximity to the Manhattan Bridge and Williamsburg Bridge, providing convenient access between Manhattan and Brooklyn. With its strategic location, flexible commercial layouts, and the opportunity to acquire a significant footprint within one of Manhattan’s most active downtown neighborhoods, the Property presents a compelling opportunity for investors and users seeking scale and long-term value in a supply-constrained market. Property tours are by appointment only. Please contact the listing broker to schedule.
