    Condo Description

    Building 01 is a two-story office building of 3,840 square feet at Palmera, a new campus of twelve office buildings for sale on SH 121 at Dijon Street in West Plano. The building has a 48' × 40' footprint, with 1,920 square feet on each floor. It’s one of the largest buildings on the campus, and it stands on its own with no shared walls. It sits on the east side of the campus, between Buildings 13 and 02, with green space and the existing channel behind it, which gives it one of the quieter, more open settings on the site. It has its own front entrance and its own signage, with no shared lobby or hallways. Parking is a few steps from the door. The design is coastal-inspired: white stucco, a metal roof and black shutters. The campus around it has a stamped-concrete parking court, coastal landscaping and a café and market on site. Building 01 suits a law firm, CPA, wealth advisor, insurance agency or another professional practice that meets clients in person. The campus sits within Mustang Square, minutes from Legacy West, Toyota North America’s headquarters, Stonebriar Centre and the Dallas North Tollway. More than 347,000 residents and 175,000 daytime employees are within five miles.

    Executive Summary

    Palmera: Own Your Office Building in West Plano Palmera is a new campus of twelve standalone, two-story office buildings for sale on SH 121 at Dijon Street in West Plano. It is now pre-selling, with delivery planned for Q4 2027. Each building sells fee simple, so you own the building and the land under it. This isn’t a condo suite or a share of someone else’s building. Buildings range from 2,080 to 3,905 square feet. Each has its own front door, its own signage and no shared lobby or hallways. Early buyers can choose their finishes. The campus is designed with the character of a Gulf Coast town: white stucco buildings with metal roofs and black shutters, set around a stamped-concrete parking court with coastal landscaping and tree-lined walks. A café and market on site, in Building 06, gives tenants a place for coffee, lunch or an informal meeting. The site covers 3.51 acres within Mustang Square. Its entire north edge faces the Sam Rayburn Tollway, which gives every building strong visibility and easy access. Legacy West, Toyota North America’s headquarters, Stonebriar Centre and the Dallas North Tollway are all within about 10 minutes. DFW and Love Field are each under 25 minutes away. More than 347,000 residents and 175,000 daytime employees are within five miles. Palmera suits firms that meet clients in person, such as law, accounting, wealth management, insurance, real estate and mortgage, architecture and engineering, consulting, and wellness practices. Owner-occupants may qualify for SBA 504 financing, which can require much less money down than a conventional commercial loan. Buyers can also hold a building as an investment and lease it out. Developed by Foundry Capital, with Holz + Stein as general contractor and Boerder-Snyder Architecture as architect. Contact us for pricing, floor plans and availability: (469) 294-9762 | Info@FoundryCapitalRe.com
