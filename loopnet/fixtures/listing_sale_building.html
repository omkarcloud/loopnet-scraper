    Executive Summary

    Lee & Associates – Los Angeles West is pleased to present 8925 W Olympic Blvd, a rare covered land opportunity in the heart of Beverly Hills. Located just south of the Golden Triangle and blocks from the Purple Line Extension, the offering includes a ±4,959 SF vacant retail building on a ±12,281 SF lot, recently improved with over $1.75 million in capital upgrades. Zoned C-3T-2 within the Mixed-Use Overlay Zone (MUOZ), the site supports multiple strategies, including luxury mixed-use residential, medical office with retail, or a structured parking facility—each tailored to the submarket’s strong demand drivers. For investors, developers, or owner-users, the property offers immediate functionality with long-term upside, combining location, flexibility, and entitlement potential in one of Los Angeles’ most supply-constrained and enduringly valuable investment corridors.
